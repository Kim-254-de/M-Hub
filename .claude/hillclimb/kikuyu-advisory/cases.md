## Case details

### explain-01

Case facts given to the adviser:

````text
Crop: tomato.
Diagnosis confirmed by a verified agrovet: Late blight.
Share of plants affected when reported: some.
Ward: Kirimara.
Reviewed first steps already given to the farmer:
- Remove leaves and fruit with spots. Bury them or burn them away from the field.
- Water at the base of the plants in the morning. Do not pour water over the leaves.
- Do not work among the plants while they are wet.
````

- English: What is this late blight disease? Where did it come from?
- Kiswahili: Huu ugonjwa wa baa chelewa ni nini? Umetoka wapi?
- A good answer: Simple explanation: a mould-like disease spread by spores in wind and rain splash, worse in cool wet weather; no product named.
